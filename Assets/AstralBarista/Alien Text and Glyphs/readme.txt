Alien Text and Glyphs
======================

v.1.0
CHANGE LOG
- First release
- 66 seperate Unity custom fonts
- 6 font sets containing a total of 100 characters
- Monitor example using Glow text effect
- Original Adobe Illustrator files included in current CC and legacyand CS5 filetypes,


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------


A collection of 100 glyphs and over 700 sprites that can be used as alien, scifi or ancient text, symbols, puzzles.

6 sets of glyphs/characters each with many variations in style (glow, drop shadow, embossed, etc) all available as individual mages, sprite sheets and Unity custom font variations so you can use them in GUIs, textures, materials etc.

Includes graphical variations of each from pixelart, to 200 x 200 that can be used on screen in-game, or as large textures on walls as part of puzzles to be solved? a trail? floor or wall markings, ancient cave paintings, whatever your game/app and imagination wants!


Variations including...
• Black
• Black embossed effect
• Grey embossed
• White
• White Embossed
• White Glow
• White Shadow
• Pixelart Black
• Pixelart White
• Pixelart White Embossed
• Pixelart White Glow


IDEAS
• Overlap 'glow' variations of fonts for a more prominent glow effect
• Recolour the white and grey variations infinitly for maximum variation
• Try 'Embossed' font images as normal maps for a truly embossed effect for wall etchings, runes, engravings etc
• Good for custom keyboards, secret codes, keypads etc
• Including original .ai files (in CC and CS5 formats for maximum compatibility) for resizing, OR just ask and we can update to include your requested sizes
• Also SVG formats included if required.
• We let YOU recreate the sprites in any resolution using the included illustrator files (vector graphics)
• Share ideas and tips by commenting or contact us directly to help improve this asset.
• All included scenes are samples for inspiration purposes only.  This asset is intended as artwork for coders and developers.
